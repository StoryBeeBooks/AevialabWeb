Benefit 01

Catch health issues before they become emergencies.

Your dog can't tell you when something's wrong. But patterns can. When you log every meal, walk, and bathroom break, you'll spot the subtle changes that signal trouble — like soft stools appearing 6 hours after a specific food, or decreased appetite before anyone notices lethargy. Present your vet with objective data instead of trying to remember when symptoms started.

  • Spot food allergens by correlating stool consistency with specific ingredients over time.
  • Identify early signs of illness before they escalate to emergency vet visits.
  • Track symptom patterns your entire care team observes throughout the day.
  • Export complete health reports showing exactly what happened and when.

How it works: The unified timeline consolidates observations from everyone caring for your dog. Stool quality scores (1-7 scale), meal logs, and symptom notes create a complete clinical picture your veterinarian can actually use.

Benefit 02

Never worry about missed medications or double-doses.

Did someone already give the morning insulin? Is it time for the arthritis pill? When multiple people care for your dog, medication errors are terrifyingly easy. One family member logs the dose in the caregiver app, and you instantly see it confirmed on your dashboard. No more frantic texts. No more dangerous mistakes.

  • See exactly who gave which medication and when, across your entire household.
  • Prevent dangerous double-dosing with real-time medication logs.
  • Track compliance for chronic conditions your vet needs to monitor.
  • Set up your dog walker or sitter to administer meds while you're away, with full visibility.

How it works: Caregivers using Aevia Pup Partner log medications with exact dosages. These entries route to your secure review queue where you approve them, creating an unbroken record of every dose administered.

Benefit 03

Stop overfeeding, start managing weight properly.

Everyone loves giving treats. The problem? When five people each give "just a few treats," your dog quietly gains 15% body weight before anyone realizes. See exactly how many calories your dog actually consumes — including every treat, meal, and "just a bite" — calculated against their scientifically-determined metabolic needs.

  • Know your dog's precise caloric needs based on weight, breed, age, and activity level.
  • See total daily calories including treats logged by everyone in your care network.
  • Track weight trends over weeks and months to catch gradual changes.
  • Get your dog to a healthy weight with data, not guesswork.

How it works: The metabolic engine calculates Resting Energy Requirement (RER) and Maintenance Energy Requirement (MER) using veterinary formulas, adjusting for 82 breed-specific factors, life stages, and activity levels. Every meal and treat logged by your care network counts toward the daily total.

Benefit 04

Coordinate your entire care team without sharing passwords.

Your dog walker notices limping. Your pet sitter sees decreased appetite. Your teenager forgets to mention the vomiting. When everyone uses different group chats and sticky notes, critical information gets lost. Give your whole care network a secure way to report what they observe — without access to your personal data, billing info, or other dogs' records.

  • Dog walkers log walks, stool quality, and behavioral observations in real-time.
  • Pet sitters track exact meal portions and water intake while you're traveling.
  • Family members document symptoms and medications without confusion.
  • Review and approve every entry before it becomes part of the official record.

How it works: Generate secure invitation links with specific permissions (walks only, meals only, vet care, etc.). Caregivers download Aevia Pup Partner, sign in with their Apple ID, and submit observations. You review entries in your private queue and approve what goes into your dog's timeline — all with zero-knowledge encryption.

Your Data Stays Yours

Built for privacy, designed for families.

Your dog's health data is stored entirely on your device. Caregivers can't access your personal information, billing details, or other pets' records. All data syncs through Apple's private CloudKit — we can't read, access, or recover your information under any circumstances.
