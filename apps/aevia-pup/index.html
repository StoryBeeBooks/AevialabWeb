01 · Metabolic Intelligence

Precision nutrition engineered for your dog.

Unlike generic pet apps that calculate caloric needs based on broad weight brackets, Aevia Pup features a precise metabolic engine built on veterinary science. The app computes your dog's Resting Energy Requirement (RER) using the formula RER = 70 × (Weight in kg)^0.75, then overlays adjusting coefficients for life stage, activity thresholds, and breed-specific multipliers from our curated 82-breed database.

  • Mathematical RER & MER calculations tailored to your dog's weight, age, and activity level.
  • Adaptive nutrition planning for intact vs neutered adults, puppy growth phases, gestation, lactation, and weight management.
  • Breed-specific metabolic multipliers for 82 recognized breeds.
  • Dynamic targets that adjust automatically as your dog's profile evolves.

02 · Unified Timeline

Every observation, one chronological ledger.

Aevia Pup eliminates fragmented tracking by consolidating every aspect of your dog's day into a single, cohesive chronological feed. Dietary intake, hydration events, weight entries, stool quality indexes, localized symptom records, and medication schedules all appear in a unified calendar view. Pattern correlation lets you view the exact relationship between clinical events and dietary intake, helping you identify environmental or dietary triggers before they become chronic problems.

  • Comprehensive metrics: meals, hydration, weight, stool consistency, symptoms, and medications in one view.
  • Pattern correlation tools to identify triggers and trends over time.
  • Navigate by day, week, or month to review historical data.
  • Export structured PDF reports for veterinary consultations.

03 · Collaborative Care

Your entire care network, synchronized securely.

In modern households, caring for a dog is rarely a solitary task. Dog walkers, professional sitters, family helpers, and roommates all contribute to your dog's wellbeing. Aevia Pup connects your entire care network through a secure, zero-trust gatekeeper protocol. Caregivers use the companion app (Aevia Pup Partner: Caregivers) to submit observations, which route to your secure Review Queue where you can inspect, approve, or reject entries before they're committed to your dog's official history.

  • Generate scope-restricted invitations for dog walkers, sitters, and family helpers.
  • Review Queue (Inbox) for auditing caregiver submissions before committing to the timeline.
  • Dynamic scope isolation: restrict access to specific duties (walks only, vet care only, etc.).
  • Zero-knowledge transit: caregivers cannot access your personal accounts or private data.

04 · Clinical Reporting

Objective data for your veterinarian.

During diagnostic consultations, veterinarians are often forced to rely on subjective, retrospective owner recall. Aevia Pup translates subjective memory into objective, structured data. Export a comprehensive PDF Clinical Report showing multi-caregiver tracking ledgers, stool consistency scores correlated with dietary changes, stable body weight records, and strict medication compliance logs. Armed with this detailed, reliable data, your veterinarian can make confident diagnostic decisions without costly exploratory testing.

  • Export structured PDF Clinical Reports for vet visits.
  • True caloric trajectories plotted against computed RER and MER baselines.
  • Digestive consistency trends correlated directly with dietary changes.
  • Symptom and treatment timelines removing guesswork from therapeutic compliance.

Privacy-First Architecture

Zero-knowledge security, absolute data sovereignty.

Aevia Pup is built under a strict "privacy-first, local-first" philosophy. Your dog's personal information is protected by aggressive client-side sandboxing and modern cryptographic protocols. All data is stored locally on your iOS device, with optional private CloudKit backups managed entirely by Apple.

Support & FAQ Privacy Policy Terms of Use
Local SwiftData Sandboxing
Your dog's profiles, histories, weight logs, and photographs are stored in an encrypted database directly on your physical iOS device.
Zero-Knowledge Transit
Caregiver logs are encrypted on-device via AES-256-GCM using an ephemeral 32-byte key. The key is shared directly with caregivers via URL fragment, never traversing our servers.
Private Cloud Backups
Optional synchronization uses Apple's private CloudKit container. West Solution Consulting Corp. cannot access, view, or restore your records under any circumstances.
At-Will Revocation
Instantly revoke caregiver access tokens, rendering future uploads unauthorized and wiping matching entries from transit databases.
