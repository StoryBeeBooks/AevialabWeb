1.1 Data Stored Exclusively On Your Device (Local Storage)

The vast majority of the data you enter or generate within the Main App or Partner App is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your physical device unless you explicitly enable cloud features. This includes:

  • Dog Profiles & Physical Metrics: Names, breeds, sexes, birthdates, activity coefficients, and weight histories.
  • Nutrition & Hydration Logs: Daily food records, ingredient names, treat calorie entries, and water intake records.
  • Clinical Health Records: Symptom logs, physical severity indicators, medication doses, and vaccination dates.
  • Captures & Images: Photos captured or imported to document physical symptoms or meals.
  • Local Insight Computations: All algorithmic calorie target calculations (RER/MER) and food safety checks are executed entirely on-device using bundled, offline reference databases.

1.2 Data Backed Up via Your Private Cloud (Private Cloud Storage)

  • CloudKit Synchronization: If you enable iCloud backup in the Main App or Partner App settings, iOS securely replicates your local SwiftData database to your personal Apple iCloud account via CloudKit.
  • Company Access Limit: This synchronization pipeline is managed entirely by Apple Inc. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Apple's security protocols. West Solution Consulting Corp. does not operate these servers and has no administrative ability to access, read, or restore this data.

1.3 Data Routed via Our Infrastructure (Encrypted Transit Data)

To coordinate authentication and caregiver collaboration, the Service communicates with our secure infrastructure (hosted on Supabase) using the following limited data points:

  • Apple Sign-In Identifiers (SIWA): We utilize Sign in with Apple for secure, anonymous authentication. The App transmits your secure Apple identity token to our verification endpoint (verify-siwa) to validate your session and generate a stable, anonymous user identifier (the Apple subject string). We do not collect your real name or primary email address.
  • Hashed Invitation Tokens: When an Owner generates a sharing invite, the raw, random 32-byte secret token is hashed on-device using SHA-256. Only the SHA-256 hash (token_hash) is stored on our servers.
  • Caregiver Verification Metadata: When a Caregiver claims an invite, their anonymous Apple ID subject string (contributor_apple_sub) and their chosen display name (contributor_display_name) are saved to verify their subsequent data uploads.
  • Encrypted Submissions (The Caregiver Inbox): When a Caregiver logs an activity, the payload is encrypted on-device. The resulting ciphertext (base64-encoded) is uploaded to our partner_inbox table alongside unencrypted routing metadata:
    • dog_id (to match the profile)
    • dog_name (for owner interface display)
    • category (one of: walk, meal, or vet, to enforce scope validation)
    • client_event_id (a client-generated UUID used to prevent duplicate uploads)
    • created_at & expires_at (timestamps to manage queue cleanup)

2. Cryptographic Architecture of Caregiver Collaboration

To ensure complete data privacy, the sharing mechanism between the Main App and the Partner App is designed as a zero-knowledge transit system.

2.1 Cryptographic Key Custody

  • Client-Side Key Generation: When an Owner creates a caregiver invitation, the Main App generates a high-entropy, 32-byte symmetric AES-256 key entirely on-device using secure system APIs (SecRandomCopyBytes).
  • The URL Fragment Rule: This AES key is appended to the generated invite link as a URL fragment (#key=base64url...). Under internet protocol standard RFC 3986, URL fragments are processed strictly client-side by the operating system and are never sent to web servers or network routers.
  • Zero Server Knowledge: We do not receive, store, or transmit the decryption key. Consequently, our servers hold only unreadable base64-encoded ciphertext. We cannot decrypt or read your caregiver's logs.

2.2 Data Transit and Automatic Erasure

  • Temporary Inbox Storage: Encrypted caregiver submissions are held in our secure intermediate database (partner_inbox) as an ephemeral queue.
  • Owner-Initiated Erasure: When an Owner approves or rejects an inbox entry via the Pending Review panel, the corresponding database row is permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Automatic TTL Purge: Any submission left unreviewed by an Owner is automatically swept and permanently deleted by our database maintenance scheduler (pg_cron) after seven (7) days.

3. How We Use Information

Because we do not have custody of your dog's health, nutrition, or physical logs, we cannot use your data for advertising, machine learning model training, or behavioral profiling.

The minimal technical identifiers we process are used exclusively to:

  • Validate Sessions: Authenticate active devices and secure API access using anonymous Sign in with Apple signatures.
  • Route Secure Payloads: Verify cryptographic tokens, enforce caregiver scopes, and route encrypted payloads to the correct Owner's review queue.
  • Send Push Notifications: Trigger real-time Apple Push Notification service (APNs) alerts via database triggers when a new, encrypted caregiver log is submitted.
  • Improve Platform Stability: Log anonymous session event metadata (if you have opted in) to detect technical crashes and app store performance anomalies.

5.1 Self-Serve Data Erasure

You can trigger a complete database wipe at any time from within the app's settings panel under Settings > Account > Delete Account (Main App) or Settings > Account > Delete Account & Data (Partner App). Once triggered, the following events execute immediately:

  1. Local SwiftData Wipe: Erases all local database records, dog profiles, weight histories, and caregiver caches on your physical device.
  2. Private Cloud Purge: Instructs your private Apple CloudKit container to delete all mirrored Aevia Pup data.
  3. Database Clearing: Instructs our servers to permanently delete your anonymous Apple user subject identifier, revoke all active caregiver invitation tokens, and delete any pending encrypted logs from our databases.

Note: This deletion process is complete and irreversible. Because we do not hold copies of your decryption keys or maintain server-side backups, we cannot recover your data once deleted.

6. Security Practices

We employ aggressive, industry-standard protocols to protect your data:

  • Secure Enclave Integration: All session tokens and cryptographic keys are stored in the secure iOS Keychain, protected by system-level hardware encryption.
  • Minimal API Surface Area: We have stripped out all third-party tracking APIs to ensure deterministic execution and prevent unauthorized data leaks.
  • Enforced Transport Security: All client-to-server communications are conducted exclusively over TLS (HTTPS) with pinned endpoints where applicable.
