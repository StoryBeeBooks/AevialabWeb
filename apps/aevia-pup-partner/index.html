01 · Dog Walkers

Activity tracking and digestive diagnostics.

Dog walkers log walk durations in real-time or post-facto, recording the exact time, duration, and pace of each outing. Using the integrated Bristol-inspired stool metric, walkers document stool consistency on a 1–7 scale (from hard, dry pellets to liquid diarrhea) and note physical anomalies such as foreign body ingestion or discoloration. These indices are transmitted immediately to the Owner's app, allowing continuous monitoring of digestive health and exercise consistency throughout the work week.

  • Precise activity tracking with real-time or retroactive walk logging.
  • Integrated 1–7 Bristol stool consistency scale for digestive diagnostics.
  • Log physical anomalies, foreign body ingestion, and behavioral observations.
  • Instant transmission to Owner's review queue for timeline integration.

02 · Pet Sitters

Nutrition tracking and hydration auditing.

Professional sitters document exact food weights in grams and food categories (kibble, wet food, or treats), ensuring the dog's daily caloric targets are met. Hydration auditing allows sitters to log water intake in milliliters, helping Owners identify early indicators of metabolic changes, renal strain, or urinary tract issues. The app operates fully offline, allowing sitters in remote areas or high-density buildings with poor cell signal to continue logging without interruption. Entries cache locally and synchronize when connectivity is restored.

  • Calculated meal tracking with exact food weights in grams.
  • Food category classification: kibble, wet food, and treats.
  • Hydration auditing with milliliter-precise water intake logging.
  • Offline resilience: cache entries locally and sync when connection returns.

03 · Family Helpers

Clinical compliance and symptom monitoring.

Family helpers and veterinary assistants record precise pharmaceutical interventions, logging the compound name, timing, and exact dosage in milligrams or milliliters. Prevent dangerous double-medication errors with synchronized dose logs visible across the entire household. Document physical symptoms—vomiting, coughing, lethargy, mobility issues—with standardized clinical severity scores (1 to 5), ensuring the Owner's timeline contains objective observations that can be shared with veterinarians during diagnostic consultations.

  • Medication administration logs with compound name, timing, and exact dosage.
  • Prevent double-medication errors with synchronized household visibility.
  • Symptom monitoring with standardized 1–5 clinical severity scores.
  • Objective observations for veterinary diagnostic consultations.

04 · Clinical Integration

From caregiver input to veterinary diagnosis.

The Partner App completes a powerful reporting loop designed to support veterinary clinical evaluations. Caregivers across your distributed network log observations using the Partner App. These entries route securely to the Owner's review queue in Aevia Pup: Dog Care & Diary. The Owner audits and approves entries, integrating them into the dog's official timeline. During veterinary consultations, the Owner exports a structured PDF Clinical Report showing true caloric trajectories, digestive consistency trends correlated with dietary changes, and comprehensive symptom and treatment timelines.

  • Decentralized observation: distributed caregivers log in real-time.
  • Owner audit and timeline integration via secure review queue.
  • Veterinary synthesis: export comprehensive PDF health reports.
  • Remove guesswork from therapeutic compliance reviews.

05 · Getting Started

Join a care network in three steps.

Getting started with Aevia Pup Partner is simple. Download the app from the App Store on your iOS or iPadOS 18.0+ device. Accept the secure Universal Link invitation sent by the dog's Owner (e.g., https://aevialab.com/apps/aevia-pup-partner/join?token=...#key=...). Complete Sign in with Apple and set your preferred display name so the Owner knows exactly who logged each observation. Access your personalized dashboard to view authorized dogs and start recording walks, meals, medications, and symptoms immediately.

  • Download the app: iOS and iPadOS 18.0+ compatible.
  • Accept the invite: open the secure Universal Link from the Owner.
  • Configure your identity: Sign in with Apple and set your display name.
  • Begin collaborative care: access authorized dogs and start logging.

Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

Collaborative care without compromising security.

Aevia Pup Partner is designed with advanced cryptographic separations to ensure collaborative care does not compromise household security. Caregivers sign in with their own Apple ID, keeping identities independent. All logs are encrypted on-device via AES-256-GCM, with symmetric keys shared directly via URL fragments that never touch our servers.

No Shared Logins
Caregivers sign in with their own Apple ID via Sign in with Apple, keeping identities independent of the Owner's account.
Zero-Knowledge Relay
All caregiver logs are encrypted on-device via AES-256-GCM. The symmetric key is shared directly via URL fragment and never touches our servers.
Encrypted Transit Conduit
We act purely as an encrypted transit conduit. We cannot read, decrypt, or analyze the observations you transmit.
At-Will Revocation
Owners can instantly revoke access tokens, rendering future uploads unauthorized and wiping matching entries from transit databases.
