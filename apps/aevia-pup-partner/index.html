Benefit 01

Provide better service with less effort.

Stop texting updates, taking photos of food portions, or scribbling notes the owner might never see. Log everything directly in the app while you're on the walk or feeding the dog. The owner gets real-time updates without you needing to send messages or remember details hours later. Show professionalism that sets you apart.

  • Log walks, meals, and bathroom breaks in seconds while you're actually doing them.
  • Owners see your updates instantly without waiting for your text message.
  • Stand out as a professional caregiver with detailed, reliable documentation.
  • Spend more time with the dog, less time writing update messages.

How it works: The owner invites you via a secure link. You sign in with your Apple ID, and you'll only see the dogs you're authorized to care for. Your logs sync instantly to the owner's timeline.

Benefit 02

Protect yourself from misunderstandings.

"Did you give him breakfast?" "How long was the walk?" "Was there anything unusual?" Stop defending yourself against forgotten instructions or unclear expectations. When everything you do is logged with timestamps, there's a clear record showing exactly what happened, when, and how. No more he-said-she-said situations.

  • Timestamped records prove exactly when you walked, fed, or medicated the dog.
  • Document unusual symptoms or behaviors the moment you notice them.
  • Show professionalism with complete, accurate care records.
  • Protect yourself from liability with clear documentation of what you did and observed.

How it works: Every entry includes who logged it, exactly when, and all relevant details. The owner reviews and approves entries, creating an official record of care that protects both of you.

Benefit 03

Never miss important health signs.

You're often the first person to notice something's wrong — soft stool, decreased appetite, unusual lethargy. But if you just mention it casually and the owner forgets, that early warning gets lost. Log what you observe in the moment, with clinical detail, so the owner and their vet have the information they need to act fast.

  • Record stool consistency on a 1-7 clinical scale the moment you see it.
  • Document behavioral changes like limping, coughing, or decreased energy.
  • Note exactly what and how much the dog ate or refused to eat.
  • Help catch serious health issues before they become emergencies.

How it works: The app includes standardized scales for stool quality (1-7) and symptom severity (1-5). Your clinical observations become part of the dog's permanent health timeline that veterinarians actually use.

Benefit 04

Work offline, sync later.

Walking dogs in a park with spotty cell service? Caring for a dog in a rural area with no signal? The app works completely offline. Log everything you need during the walk or visit, and it'll automatically sync to the owner's app when you're back in range. Never lose data. Never wait for connectivity.

  • Log walks, meals, and observations with zero connectivity required.
  • All entries save locally and sync automatically when signal returns.
  • Never lose data or wait around for your phone to connect.
  • Perfect for remote boarding facilities, rural properties, or dense urban areas with poor signal.

How it works: The app caches all entries locally on your device. Once connectivity is restored, encrypted logs sync securely to the owner's review queue without any action from you.

Benefit 05

Simple, secure, and totally private.

You don't get access to the owner's personal information, billing details, other pets, or family data. You see only the dogs you're authorized to care for, and only the actions you're allowed to log. Sign in once with your Apple ID, accept the invitation link, and you're ready to work. No passwords to remember. No accounts to create.

  • Sign in once with Apple ID — no usernames, passwords, or account setup.
  • See only the dogs you're authorized to care for, nothing else.
  • Scope-restricted access: walkers log walks, sitters log meals, family logs everything.
  • Zero-knowledge encryption protects the data you're logging from everyone, including us.

How it works: The owner generates a secure invitation link with specific permissions (walks only, meals only, vet care, etc.). You download the app, accept the invite, and see only what you're authorized to see. All logs are encrypted on your device before being sent.
